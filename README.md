## 🌍 About SRE UPNVJ

**Society of Renewable Energy (SRE) UPN "Veteran" Jakarta** is a student-led organization dedicated to accelerating renewable energy awareness, clean technology, and sustainable impact among youth and communities in Indonesia.

---

## 💻 IT Ecosystem Division

**IT Ecosystem** is the technology and digital product division under the **Operations Department** of SRE UPNVJ. We build, maintain, and scale the organization's official web presence and digital infrastructure so SRE UPNVJ's initiatives, publications, and programs are accessible to students, partners, and the wider public.

### What We Build
- **Official Website (`sreupnvj.id`)** — The central digital hub for SRE UPNVJ's profile, divisions, impact stories, press releases, blog, programs, and events.
- **Digital Ecosystem & Tooling** — Fast, reliable, and edge-powered web tools that support cross-division collaboration and community engagement.
